1. Our Artist Privacy Pledge

Artifex Academy Inc. ("Artifex", "Artifex Atelier", "we", "us", or "our") operates the online art and illustration learning platform accessible at artifexacademy.com and its affiliated digital services. We are dedicated to providing a transformative, safe, and supportive educational space for traditional and digital artists across the world.

Unlike commercial social media platforms, our business model is simple: students pay tuition for world-class art instruction, structured curriculum, and personalized mentor critique. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal data or artwork to third-party advertisers or AI model training syndicates.

✓ Core Atelier Guarantee

Your Artwork Remains Exclusively Yours

Every thumbnail, gesture sketch, finished digital painting, watercolor scan, and written artist note submitted to Artifex Academy remains the sole intellectual property of the student creator. You grant Artifex only the minimal technical license needed to deliver video draw-over critiques and display your piece in our public showcase if you choose to opt in.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, as well as technical information necessary to maintain a seamless learning environment:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Student Profile & Contact: When you register for an account, we collect your full name, email address, preferred art mediums (e.g., watercolor, digital painting), skill level, and personal artist bio.
  • Creative Artwork Submissions: Image files (JPG, PNG, TIFF, PSD, scans) uploaded to the assignment portal, accompanying artist statements, revision iterations, and questions for instructors.
  • Billing & Subscription Details: Billing address, plan selection (Beginner Studio, All-Access Atelier, Master Mentorship), and invoice history. Note: Payment card numbers are processed via certified PCI-DSS compliant gateways; Artifex never stores raw credit card numbers.
  • Communications: Messages submitted via our admissions enquiry form, student desk emails, or Discord community interactions.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Course Progress & Telemetry: Video lesson playback timestamps, completed module checkmarks, assignment submission dates, and critique read receipts.
  • Device & Browser Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, display resolution, and language preference to ensure optimal image and video rendering.
  • Interface Preferences: Local storage flags for Dark/Light mode, LTR/RTL text orientation, and dismissed studio announcements.

3. How We Use Student Data

Artifex Academy processes your personal information strictly for the following educational and operational purposes:

  • Educational Delivery: Providing high-definition video lesson streams, downloadable brush libraries (.ABR files), reference photography packs, and interactive modules.
  • Faculty Mentorship & Critiques: Enabling working artists (such as Elena Rostova, Mateo Silva, and Suki Chen) to review your submitted artwork, record visual draw-overs, and provide written feedback.
  • Student Showcase Display (Optional): Featuring your approved assignments in the public Student Showcase Gallery only when you enable the "Public Showcase" visibility toggle.
  • Account Administration: Processing tuition subscriptions, sending invoice receipts, notifying you of upcoming assignment deadlines, and responding to support tickets.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our student portal against unauthorized access, malicious attacks, and community conduct violations.

4. Student Artwork & Intellectual Property Rights

Because art education relies on personal creative expression, we establish clear and unwavering legal boundaries regarding your creative output:

A. Absolute Student Ownership

You retain all copyright, moral rights, and commercial ownership of all original artwork, illustrations, concepts, designs, and sketches submitted to Artifex Academy. Submitting an assignment does not transfer any proprietary rights to us.

B. Limited Educational License

By uploading an assignment, you grant Artifex Academy a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free license solely to:

  • Display the artwork privately to your assigned faculty instructor for grading, annotation, and draw-overs.
  • Store a high-resolution preview in your private Student Dashboard portfolio archive.
  • If—and only if—you maintain the "Public Showcase" setting enabled, display the artwork in our curated student showcase gallery with full credit to your artist name. You may revoke this permission at any moment via your dashboard settings.

C. Zero AI Training Pledge

Artifex Academy will never license, sell, or feed student artwork, drawings, sketches, or assignment uploads into generative AI training datasets, scraping pools, or external machine learning algorithms.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data and creative assets:

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All web traffic, video streams, and file transfers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols.
  • Role-Restricted Administration: Faculty mentors have access only to the assignments and student profiles assigned to their course rosters. Administrator access is restricted to verified platform directors.
  • Client-Side LocalStorage: Interface preferences (such as Dark/Light theme, RTL layout, and client-side simulation state) are persisted locally in your web browser and never transmitted to external third parties.
  • Simulated Demo Safeguards: In frontend demonstration environments, sample billing and authentication operations do not interact with live financial accounts, ensuring safe testing without risk of unexpected charges.

6. Cookies & Local Preferences

Artifex Academy uses minimal, privacy-friendly storage technologies:

  • Essential Session Storage: Necessary to keep you logged in to your student dashboard and remember your lesson player position.
  • Preference Storage: Storing your selected color theme (Light vs. Dark Mode) and reading direction (LTR vs. RTL) via standard browser localStorage.
  • No Third-Party Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy invasive third-party ad retargeting trackers, Facebook tracking pixels, or data broker beacons.

7. Your Global Privacy Rights

Regardless of where you reside, Artifex Academy honors comprehensive privacy rights compliant with GDPR (Europe), CCPA/CPRA (California), UK Data Protection Act, and global standards:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request an export of your personal information, submission history, and instructor feedback at any time.
  • Right of Correction: You can modify your artist name, email, bio, and medium preferences directly in the Profile & Settings view of your dashboard.
  • Right to Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Upon request, we will permanently delete your account, personal data, and remove any submitted artworks from our servers.
  • Right to Opt Out: You can unsubscribe from our Studio Dispatch newsletter with one click, or toggle off showcase visibility at will.
