Digital Illustration & Painting

Deep-dive training in Photoshop, Procreate, and Clip Studio Paint. Learn brush economy, layering psychology, gamut masking, and cinematic rendering under feature film veterans.

Key Learning Outcomes:
  • Build dynamic silhouettes and controlled greyscale value structures
  • Master digital brush customization, textures, and edge hierarchy
  • Render complex material surfaces (reflective metals, skin, fabric drape)

Service 03

Traditional Art: Watercolor, Drawing & Oils

Connect with physical pigments and paper. Experience classical sight-size graphite drawing, luminous granulating watercolor washes, and expressive acrylic painting techniques.

Key Learning Outcomes:
  • Control water-to-pigment saturation timing without muddying washes
  • Draw human anatomy accurately with classical Renaissance sight-size methods
  • Layer mixed media, walnut inks, and gouache with tactile authenticity
Skill Level: All Levels (Beginner to Experienced)
