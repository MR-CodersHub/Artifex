2. Atelier Code of Conduct

Art education thrives on honest feedback, psychological safety, and vulnerable experimentation. Every student, instructor, and staff member must adhere to our Studio Principles:

The Artifex Studio Honor Pledge

Constructive Critique & Mutual Respect

When evaluating fellow students' drawings in community Discord channels or atelier workshops, prioritize compassionate, actionable critique. Focus on form, lighting, composition, and values—never personal insults, gatekeeping, or condescension.

Prohibited Studio Behaviors:

  • Plagiarism & Art Theft: Submitting drawings, paintings, 3D assets, or photographic studies created by third parties and falsely claiming them as your own original coursework.
  • Harassment & Discriminatory Conduct: Any form of hate speech, misogyny, racism, sexual harassment, or derogatory comments in study halls, reviews, or private messages will result in immediate termination of account access without refund.
  • Account Sharing & Screen Scraping: Access credentials and streaming tokens are strictly individual. Distributing, recording, torrenting, or rebroadcasting Artifex course videos or lesson assets is strictly forbidden and constitutes a federal copyright violation.
  • Commercial Solicitation: Unsolicited advertising, spamming, or marketing independent commercial goods within course critique channels.

4. Student Artwork Ownership & Intellectual Rights

We honor the dignity of the artist. Your artwork belongs to you:

  • 100% Student Copyright: You retain complete, unencumbered intellectual property rights, commercial exploitation rights, and moral rights over every piece of original artwork, sketch, gesture study, character design, and finished painting you create and submit.
  • No Automatic Commercial Rights to Artifex: Artifex Academy does not acquire any right to manufacture merchandise, sell prints, or license your original work to third parties.
  • Showcase Display Permission: If you elect to toggle on "Public Showcase" within your Student Dashboard, you grant us permission to feature your piece within our community gallery with prominent artist attribution. You may remove your artwork from the public gallery at any time by unchecking the showcase toggle.
  • Mastercopy Studies: When painting educational masterstudies (e.g. copying classical masters for value/lighting drills), students must credit the original master when sharing publicly.

5. Tuition, Membership Tiers & Refund Policy

Artifex Academy offers flexible learning plans designed with transparent pricing:

A. Membership Tiers & Billing

  • Beginner Studio ($24/month or $240/year): Access to 6 foundational beginner courses and community study forums.
  • All-Access Atelier ($49/month or $490/year): Unlimited streaming of all 38 courses, up to 4 monthly assignment critique submissions, and student showcase profile.
  • Master Mentorship ($89/month or $890/year): Priority 48-hour critique turnarounds, monthly 1-on-1 private video mentorship calls, and portfolio curation.

B. Automatic Renewal & Cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically at the selected monthly or annual interval. You may cancel your subscription at any time with a single click inside the Subscription & Payments tab of your Student Dashboard. Following cancellation, your course access remains active through the end of your current paid billing period.

C. 14-Day Atelier Satisfaction Pledge

If you enroll in an annual membership or individual course and find the instruction is not the right fit for your artistic journey, email admissions@artifexacademy.com within 14 calendar days of purchase for a 100% full refund, provided you have watched less than 35% of the total course video material.

6. Assignment Submission & Faculty Critique Standards

To ensure our working artist faculty can deliver world-class mentorship, assignment submissions operate under specific studio guidelines:

  • Turnaround Timelines: Standard critique reviews are completed within 48 to 72 business hours of submission. Master Mentorship students receive expedited 48-hour turnaround.
  • File Integrity & Formats: Students must submit assignments in supported graphic formats (PNG, JPG, TIFF, PSD up to 50MB). Files must be clean, free of malware, and legibly oriented.
  • Resubmissions & Revision Cycles: When an instructor issues a "Revision Requested" critique, students are encouraged to iterate on specific notes and resubmit via the dashboard.
